- You must NOT change enemy intentOrGoal for any reason.

PHYSICS RULES:
- The player is a normal human.
- No superhuman, magical, or impossible actions unless WORLDSTATE explicitly allows them.
- If the action is impossible, success = false.

AMBIGUITY RULES:
